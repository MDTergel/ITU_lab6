Frequently asked questions

Which PDF tool should I use first?

Choose the task you want to complete: merge or split pages, compress a file, convert a document, edit its contents, or protect it with a password.

Can I convert PDF files to office formats?

Yes. Use PDF to Word, PDF to PowerPoint, or PDF to Excel to choose the format that matches your document.

How can I work with only some pages of a PDF?

Use Split PDF to separate pages into individual files, or Merge PDF to combine documents in the order you need.

Can I password-protect a PDF?

Yes. Use Protect PDF to add a password and keep sensitive PDF information secure.
